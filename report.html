04 / SCENARIO LAB

Make the assumptions your own

A five-year operating model. Every input is a hypothesis, not guidance or consensus.

How the starting inputs are calculated

Sales growth = (latest annual revenue ÷ earliest annual revenue) raised to (1 ÷ elapsed years), minus 1. Operating margin = operating income ÷ revenue; the start uses the median of up to three recent annual margins. Tax rate = income tax expense ÷ positive pretax income; the start uses the median of eligible recent rates from 0% to 50%. History-based inputs are rounded to 0.1 percentage point to match the sliders. Missing or unsuitable history uses explicitly labeled illustrative inputs. Reinvestment, discount rate, and terminal growth remain judgment inputs.
