## Verified changes

- NVIDIA FY2026 current marketable securities: $51.951B, custom inline tag `nvda:MarketableSecuritiesAndEquitySecuritiesFVNI`, balance-sheet context `c-11` in the [FY2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm). The reviewed supplement requires exact CIK, annual start/end, revenue and operating-cash-flow bases; later periods and changed bases do not inherit it. It links directly to the filing and retains its review date. It is not a live custom-tag extraction service.
- Microsoft reports sales/marketing ($26.710B) and general/administrative ($7.956B) separately in [FY2026 statements](https://www.microsoft.com/investor/reports/ar26/index.html). Map the two direct standard tags separately, without synthesizing a combined SG&A value.
- Amazon FY2025 sales/marketing ($47.129B, standard `MarketingExpense` tag) and general/administrative ($11.172B) likewise remain separate, matching the [FY2025 statement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000004/amzn-20251231.htm). Marketing is labeled with its reported scope; it is not assumed to represent the same cost scope as every other company's tag.

It is not substituted for the narrower depreciation input of the optional reinvestment proxy. Missing receivables and selected short-term borrowings remain gaps.
